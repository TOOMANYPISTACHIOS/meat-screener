Benchmark: S&P % is ^GSPC over the identical window (entry-close date → current date); S&P P/L $ is your same capital unlevered in the index, no fees — i.e., what the money would have done parked in the benchmark instead of taking the trade. "vs S&P" in the totals = net trigger P/L − that. Note this means the leverage input moves only your side of the comparison: cranking L amplifies both your wins and losses against a fixed yardstick, which is exactly the point of a benchmark. Simulation, not accounting: entries at the next daily close after the trigger (the validated convention); "current" is the latest baked close; liquidation is checked against daily closes only — an intraday wick through the liq price would not show here, so real liquidation risk is understated at high leverage. Fees assume 0.18% round-trip (trade.xyz standard-mode tier-0 taker, verified from their fee docs; growth-mode markets would be ~10x cheaper) and each name's 20-day average funding held for the full period — funding regimes drift, so treat fee figures as order-of-magnitude. Rows marked LIQ'D count as −100% of capital. 20d shows null until 21 trading days have passed. Triggers recompute from the parameter bar on the screener tab — change dials there and this table follows. Spot-hold view: one buy per episode (trigger days >10 bars apart deduped), unlevered, no fees, benchmark = SPY with dividends. Two standing caveats baked into any long-horizon read: today's perp roster is a winners' list (names often got listed because they ran — survivorship flatters old entries), and the aggregate edge is tail-driven — the median episode roughly matches SPY; a minority of monsters pays for everything, so the result only holds if you take every episode, not the comfortable ones.

Conventions: adjusted daily closes (Yahoo) since 2022; signals computed at the close — the validated workflow enters at the next close. Pooled stats above use a 20-trading-day forward window and skip the warm-up period. Universe = Hyperliquid xyz (TradeXYZ) equity perps with ≥1y of price history; funding = exchange hourly history, annualized (positive = longs pay). The defaults are the parameters validated in the MAG7 research (edge +2.7pp/20d, era-robust, permutation p≈0.006); everything else you dial in here is unvalidated exploration — the pooled-stats strip recomputes honestly, but remember: the more knobs you turn, the more the numbers flatter you. Static snapshot — re-run the pipeline to refresh prices, funding and the perp list. Context tiers grade every trigger by five checks knowable at that close — S&P above its 200-day · 20d vol under 60%/yr · dip shallower than −25% off the 60d high · fewer than 2 other perps triggering the same fortnight · ≥50% of the universe above its own trend. Zero misses = Tier A → up to 3×, one = B → 2×, two or more = C → spot only; hover any tier chip for its specific downgrades. Tiering was validated on the 2023+ MAG7 record (A: +7.4%/82% win · B: +3.7%/69% · C: −2.9%/33%); the breadth check replaces the MAG7-basket check for this wider universe and is a sensible prior, not a proven rule.
